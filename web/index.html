Paracetamol / acetaminophen • US/Canada 150 µg/mL treatment line

Suspected overdose is urgent. Contact emergency services or a poison center immediately. This reference does not replace clinical assessment. Do not delay N-acetylcysteine while awaiting a calculation.

Patient-independent measurements

Valid nomogram interval: 4–24 hours.

All calculations are performed locally. No clinical data are uploaded or stored.

Clinical scope and limitations

Applicable only after acute ingestion with a reliable start time and concentration measured 4–24 hours later. Do not use for an unknown ingestion time, repeated supratherapeutic exposure or modified-release formulations. Samples obtained before 4 h cannot establish nomogram risk.

Extended-release products and drugs causing delayed gastric emptying may require repeat levels. A below-line concentration does not independently exclude hepatotoxicity or the need for treatment. This uses the US/Canada 150-line; other jurisdictions, including the UK, use different thresholds.

Reference: Dart et al., JAMA Network Open 2023 consensus statement. This software is not clinically validated or certified as a medical device.
